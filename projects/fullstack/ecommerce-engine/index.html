PROJECT OUTLINE

Goals

  • Demonstrate a full e-commerce storefront with cart, checkout, and admin workflows.
  • Success metric: complete a checkout flow in under 60 seconds.
  • Primary users: shoppers browsing the demo and developers studying the architecture.

Scope

  • Product catalog with category filters and search.
  • Shopping cart with quantity management and tax calculation.
  • Order history, mock Stripe checkout, and admin product management.

Architecture

  • Vanilla JS class-based app with localStorage persistence.
  • Mock Stripe payment flow validates card format client-side.
  • Admin dashboard reads and writes the same product store as the shop.

Milestones

  • Design product schema and storefront layout.
  • Build catalog, cart, and mock checkout flow.
  • Add order history, admin dashboard, and mobile responsiveness.

Risks

  • localStorage size limits with many orders and large product sets.
  • Mock payment validation not covering all invalid card edge cases.
  • Admin changes persisting across demo sessions confusing new visitors.

Next Steps

  • Integrate real Stripe.js for payment intent flow.
  • Add server-side inventory management with a PostgreSQL backend.
  • Plan v1.1: discount codes, product reviews, and wishlists.
